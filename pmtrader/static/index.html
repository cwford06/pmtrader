Metrics

Each market allows up to 100 contracts (≤ $100 at risk). ROI = total PnL ÷ ($100 × markets). Sharpe is per-market (mean ÷ std of market PnL); t-stat tests whether mean PnL > 0.

Idle.

Algorithm: PPO with clipped surrogate, GAE(λ=0.95), entropy bonus annealed 0.10→0.002, actor/critic MLPs (2×128, tanh). Reward = edge captured on each fill minus fees (unbiased, low-variance decomposition of realised PnL). Saved to models/ppo_agent.json on completion.
